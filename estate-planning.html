Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our Denver estate planning workshops and the Estate Planning Checkup.

Where are TekWise Partners estate planning workshops held?

Workshops are held in Denver, Colorado, in the afternoons and evenings. Call 719-413-5614 or book a call to reserve a seat.

Who prepares my estate planning documents?

If you choose to move forward after a workshop, you enroll with Integrated Trust Services, whose attorney network prepares your documents. TekWise Partners does not provide legal advice.

What is the Estate Planning Checkup?

The Estate Planning Checkup is a guided online tool that walks you through the different options around estate planning at your own pace. You can bring your answers to a workshop or appointment.

What will I learn at an estate planning workshop?

Workshops cover the difference between a will and a living trust, how probate works and how a trust can help avoid it, powers of attorney and health care directives, planning for incapacity, and keeping your plan up to date.

Does TekWise Partners give legal or tax advice?

No. TekWise Partners is a licensed insurance agency. Workshops and the Estate Planning Checkup are educational. Legal advice comes only from licensed attorneys.
