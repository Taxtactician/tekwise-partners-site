Estate planning

Workshops and tools to help you decide

Everyone deserves a say in what happens to their home, savings and family. Our free workshops and the I-Decide tool make it easier to start.

Free estate planning workshops

Learn the basics, without the pressure

Our workshops introduce families to trust-based estate planning through our partnership with Integrated Trust Services and its attorney network. They're friendly, practical and designed for people who don't speak legalese.

Workshops are held afternoons and evenings. Seating is limited, so please reserve your spot.

Reserve a seat Call 719-413-5614

What you'll learn

  • The difference between a will and a living trust
  • How probate works, and how a trust can help your family avoid it
  • Powers of attorney and health care directives
  • Planning for incapacity, not just for death
  • How to keep your plan up to date as life changes
